## 一、6 种场景类型提示词

### 1. 室内窗光（Interior Window Light）
```
cinematic interior scene, single window natural light source,
soft directional daylight streaming through large window,
dust particles floating in light beam, Tyndall effect,
deep shadows in room corners, warm ambient fill,
shallow depth of field f/1.8, 85mm portrait lens,
Kodak Portra 400 film aesthetic, warm highlights,
high contrast between window and shadow,
photorealistic, cinematic quality, 8K
```
适用：室内对话/肖像/沉思独处 | 参考：《教父》《燃烧女子的肖像》《花样年华》

---

### 2. 户外黄金时段（Outdoor Golden Hour）
```
outdoor scene, golden hour magic lighting,
warm orange sunlight at 15 degrees above horizon,
long soft shadows, rim light on subject,
lens flare from low sun, volumetric atmosphere,
silhouette potential, warm color grading teal and orange,
shot on 35mm cinematic lens, rule of thirds composition,
photorealistic, epic cinematic quality
```
适用：户外史诗/抒情/转折 | 参考：《旧日天堂》《边境杀手》《荒野猎人》

---

### 3. 夜景霓虹（Night Neon）
```
urban night scene, neon lights reflecting on wet pavement,
cyberpunk color palette, magenta and cyan neon glow,
atmospheric rain mist, bokeh neon lights background,
shot on 50mm lens f/1.4, shallow depth of field,
high contrast between neon highlights and deep shadows,
volumetric fog catching neon colors,
cinematic night photography, Blade Runner aesthetic
```
适用：都市夜景/赛博朋克/情绪爆发 | 参考：《银翼杀手》《Drive》《银翼杀手2049》

---

### 4. 阴天雾天（Overcast / Foggy）
```
overcast foggy exterior scene, diffused flat natural light,
no harsh shadows, soft even illumination,
morning mist atmospheric perspective,
desaturated muted color palette, cool blue-gray tones,
melancholic mood, contemplative atmosphere,
shallow focus suggesting depth through fog layers,
shot on 85mm lens, cinematic quality
```
适用：孤独/内省/悬疑/战争 | 参考：《囚徒》《分离》《潜行者》

---

### 5. 火光烛光（Firelight / Candlelight）
```
candlelight or firelight only scene, warm flickering orange light,
single practical light source, deep surrounding shadows,
high contrast 5:1 light ratio, dancing light shadows,
intimate close portrait, f/1.4 shallow depth,
warm color temperature 2800K, dramatic chiaroscuro,
Barry Lyndon candlelight aesthetic, oil painting quality,
cinematic photorealistic
```
适用：孤独/危险/浪漫/历史感 | 参考：《巴里·林登》《教父》《燃烧女子的肖像》

---

### 6. 宏大建筑（Grand Architecture）
```
epic architectural scene, monumental scale,
human figure dwarfed by structure, symmetrical composition,
dramatic perspective, leading lines to vanishing point,
volumetric light through high windows,
grand interior with columns and arches,
shot on 24mm wide lens, f/8 deep focus,
Kubrickian symmetry, cinematic epic quality
```
适用：权力/史诗/渺小感/命运 | 参考：《2001太空漫游》《沙丘》《布达佩斯大饭店》

## 二、5 种情绪分类提示词

### 1. 紧张（Tension）
```
tense cinematic scene, high contrast harsh lighting,
angular shadows, low angle shot emphasizing threat,
handheld camera slight shake, wide angle lens distortion,
desaturated cold palette with sharp highlights,
Dutch angle for psychological unease,
thriller film aesthetic, Roger Deakins cinematography style
```

### 2. 孤独（Loneliness）
```
solitary figure in vast environment, telephoto lens compression,
subject small in frame with large negative space,
cool desaturated palette, flat overcast light,
empty landscape or urban isolation,
silence visual metaphor, melancholic atmosphere,
cinematic wide shot, Wim Wenders aesthetic
```

### 3. 希望（Hope）
```
hopeful cinematic scene, warm golden light breaking through,
upward composition with sky or light source visible,
soft natural backlight, gentle lens flare,
warm color grading, slight overexposure on highlights,
subject in motion toward light,
Terrence Malick naturalistic style, uplifting atmosphere
```

### 4. 暴力（Violence）
```
intense violent cinematic moment, freeze frame action,
motion blur suggesting speed, extreme contrast lighting,
blood red accent in desaturated frame,
low angle dramatic shot, distorted wide angle,
harsh practical lighting, gritty texture,
MAPPA studio action aesthetic, dynamic diagonal composition,
visceral energy, impactful not gratuitous
```

### 5. 诗意（Poetic）
```
poetic visual moment, slow motion memory aesthetic,
soft diffused natural light, slight overexposure,
bokeh background, film grain texture,
warm muted color palette, gentle motion blur,
symbolic composition - mirror reflection or water ripple,
Makoto Shinkai detail in environment,
lyrical cinematic quality, emotional resonance
```

## 五、8 套打光方案（含AI提示词）

---

### 方案一：单窗光（Single Window Light）
**适用场景**：室内对话、肖像、沉思独处

| 参数 | 设置 |
|------|------|
| 焦段 | 85mm |
| 光圈 | f/1.8 ~ f/2.8 |
| 色温 | 5600K（日光窗光）|
| 主光 | 自然窗光或HMI/LED面光从窗外照入 |
| 填光 | 白色反光板，主体另一侧，填光比3:1 |
| 背景光 | 无（依赖自然衰减）|

**AI提示词**：
```
single window natural light, soft directional daylight,
one side illuminated, opposite side in deep shadow,
dust particles in light beam, Tyndall effect,
85mm portrait lens f/2.0, warm interior ambient,
Vermeer painting light quality, photorealistic
```
代表使用：《教父》会客室 · 《燃烧女子的肖像》室内绘画 · 《花样年华》公寓

---

### 方案二：旅馆灯泡（Hotel Bare Bulb）
**适用场景**：孤独/悬疑/犯罪/迷失场景

| 参数 | 设置 |
|------|------|
| 焦段 | 35mm ~ 50mm |
| 光圈 | f/2.8 ~ f/4 |
| 色温 | 2700K（钨丝暖光）|
| 主光 | 裸露白炽灯泡，头顶偏前，灯泡在画面内可见 |
| 填光 | 无（保留深阴影，光比5:1）|
| 背景光 | 极弱冷色荧光灯模拟走廊 |

**AI提示词**：
```
bare light bulb hanging practical light source,
warm 2700K tungsten light, harsh downward shadows,
motel room aesthetic, high contrast noir,
deep shadows in corners, single point light source,
50mm lens, cinematic gritty realism,
No Country for Old Men motel room lighting
```
代表使用：《无国界追凶》汽车旅馆 · 《出租车司机》廉价公寓 · 《囚徒》审讯场景

---

### 方案三：霓虹剪影（Neon Silhouette）
**适用场景**：都市夜景/赛博朋克/情绪爆发

| 参数 | 设置 |
|------|------|
| 焦段 | 50mm ~ 85mm |
| 光圈 | f/1.4 ~ f/2.0 |
| 主光（背景）| 霓虹灯管置于主体后方，强逆光 |
| 正面光 | 极弱冷色补光，仅提示面部轮廓 |
| 地面 | 洒水，霓虹光反射地面 |
| 大气 | 低量烟雾机，霓虹体积化 |

**AI提示词**：
```
neon light silhouette, strong backlit figure,
magenta and cyan neon colors, wet pavement reflection,
volumetric neon fog, urban night atmosphere,
Blade Runner 2049 or Drive aesthetic,
50mm lens f/1.4, subject in near-silhouette,
cinematic neon noir, emotionally charged
```
代表使用：《银翼杀手》 · 《Drive》 · 《银翼杀手2049》 · 《好莱坞往事》

---

### 方案四：反乌托邦单色（Dystopian Monochrome）
**适用场景**：极权/压迫/战争/历史/内心崩溃

| 参数 | 设置 |
|------|------|
| 焦段 | 24mm ~ 35mm（广角强调压迫空间）|
| 光圈 | f/4 ~ f/8（深景深，一切都逃不掉）|
| 色彩 | 后期去色或黑白处理 |
| 主光 | HMI硬光，45度侧顶位，强硬阴影 |
| 填光 | 极少，光比4:1~5:1 |
| 大气 | 雾机低量，强化空气中的压迫感 |

**AI提示词**：
```
dystopian monochrome scene, desaturated or black and white,
harsh directional industrial lighting, deep shadows,
wide angle lens emphasizing oppressive space,
authoritarian architecture, uniform gray palette,
Bleach Bypass processing, high contrast,
cold clinical atmosphere, no warmth,
1984 aesthetic or Mank black and white style
```
代表使用：《曼克》 · 《冷战》 · 《萨乌尔的儿子》 · 《灯塔》

---

### 方案五：自然天光（Natural Skylight）
**适用场景**：户外史诗/自由感/灵性时刻

| 参数 | 设置 |
|------|------|
| 焦段 | 14mm ~ 24mm（广角容纳天空和大地）|
| 光圈 | f/2.8 ~ f/4 |
| 色温 | 随自然光（晴天5600K / 阴天6500K / 黄金时段3200K）|
| 主光 | 完全自然天光，无任何人工补光 |
| 拍摄时机 | 黄金时段（日出后/日落前30-60分钟）或阴天 |
| 辅助 | 白色反光板极小量填光（不超过10%影响）|

**AI提示词**：
```
natural skylight cinematography, 100 percent natural light,
golden hour magic, soft rim light from low sun,
vast outdoor landscape, human figure vs nature scale,
Terrence Malick or Lubezki naturalistic style,
atmospheric haze and volumetric light,
wide angle epic composition, real skin tone,
no artificial light sources visible,
Days of Heaven or Revenant aesthetic
```
代表使用：《荒野猎人》 · 《旧日天堂》 · 《生命之树》 · 《游牧人生》

---

### 方案六：双色温对冲（Dual Color Temperature）
**适用场景**：内心冲突/转折/两个世界碰撞

| 参数 | 设置 |
|------|------|
| 焦段 | 35mm ~ 50mm |
| 光圈 | f/2.8 ~ f/4 |
| 暖光源 | 钨丝灯/蜡烛 3200K，从一侧 |
| 冷光源 | LED 6500K+，从另一侧 |
| 光比 | 暖:冷 = 2:1（暖侧为安全/过去）|
| 大气 | 低量雾机分隔冷暖区域 |

**AI提示词**：
```
dual color temperature scene, warm amber one side cool blue other,
split lighting warm vs cold on face,
color temperature conflict internal struggle,
warm practical light vs cold exterior moonlight,
Sicario border crossing aesthetic, teal and orange split,
cinematic chiaroscuro mixed color temperature
```
代表使用：《边境杀手》(冷暖分界) · 《寄生虫》(荧光+钨丝) · 《搏击俱乐部》(地下室)

### 方案七：月光蓝夜（Moonlight Blue Night）
**适用场景**：浪漫夜景/神秘/宁静/超自然

| 参数 | 设置 |
|------|------|
| 焦段 | 50mm ~ 85mm |
| 光圈 | f/1.4 ~ f/2.0 |
| 色温 | 主光7500K+蓝色凝胶 |
| 主光 | HMI+1/2 CTB，高位45度侧顶=月光 |
| 填光 | 极弱暖色（蜡烛/灯笼）前方可见 |
| 大气 | 薄雾 + 地面洒水反射月光 |

**AI提示词**：
```
moonlit night scene, cool blue moonlight from above,
silver-blue tone on skin and environment,
wet ground reflecting moonlight silver puddles,
thin mist catching blue light ethereal,
warm candle or lantern tiny warm accent in cold scene,
romantic mysterious nocturnal atmosphere,
Crouching Tiger bamboo forest moonlight aesthetic
```
